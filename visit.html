Good to know

Short answers to what people ask before a first visit.

What are the hours?

The salon works by appointment. Open times are on the live Booksy calendar. Call (562) 988-5787 with questions.

Do you take walk-ins?

Book ahead on Booksy so your time is held. For same-day questions, call (562) 988-5787.

Is there a deposit?

For installs, yes. Booksy notes that a consultation and deposit are required before an installation appointment is confirmed.

What should I bring?

Pictures of the look you want, for your consultation. For installs, come washed (no blow drying needed) and comfortable; snacks are welcome.
