How pricing works

  1. "From" means starting price

    Booksy lists most services with a starting price. Length, density and time can move the final number, which is confirmed at booking.

  2. Installs are priced by length and time

    Sisterlock Installation covers hair up to 4 inches, then $95 per inch. Micro Loc Installation has a $95 length addition. Loc Extensions cover up to 5 hours, then $100 per hour.

  3. Stay on schedule

    Palm-roll retwists add $10 to $20 past an 8-week maintenance schedule. The Over due Add-on ($75) is for 2 months past due maintenance.
